🧮 Solved Problems

Learn the method, not only the final answer.

Trigonometry

Opposite = 3 cm, Hypotenuse = 5 cm. Find sin θ.

sin θ = 3 / 5

Answer: 3/5

Electric Current

V = 20 V and R = 10 Ω. Find current.

I = V/R = 20/10 = 2 A

Answer: 2 A

📐 Trigonometry

1. Concept

Trigonometry explains the relationship between the sides and angles of a right-angled triangle.

             /|
            / |
           /  | Opposite
          / θ |
         /____|
        Adjacent

       Hypotenuse

2. Formulae

sin θ = Opposite / Hypotenuse
cos θ = Adjacent / Hypotenuse
tan θ = Opposite / Adjacent

3. Solved Problem

Opposite = 3 cm and Hypotenuse = 5 cm. Find sin θ.

sin θ = 3/5

Answer: 3/5

4. Exam Method

IDENTIFY → CHOOSE RATIO → SUBSTITUTE → SOLVE → CHECK

🧬 Heredity

1. Concept

Heredity is the transmission of characteristics from parents to offspring.

 PARENTS
    ↓
CHROMOSOMES
    ↓
   GENES
    ↓
  TRAITS
    ↓
OFFSPRING

2. Important Terms

  • Heredity: Transmission of traits.
  • Gene: Unit of hereditary information.
  • Variation: Differences in characteristics.
  • Trait: A characteristic of an organism.

3. Exam Focus

DEFINITION → CONCEPT → DIAGRAM → EXAMPLE → CONCLUSION
