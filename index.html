Physics — Electric Current

Concepts • Circuits • Formulas • Problems

Electric Current — Demo Lesson

Learn electric current through concepts, circuit diagrams, formulas and numerical problems.

🔋 Battery → ─── 💡 Bulb ─── → 🔋

Simple Electric Circuit

Important Formula

V = IR

V = Voltage
I = Current
R = Resistance

Problem Practice

Problem:
A resistor of 5 Ω is connected to a 10 V source. Find the current through the resistor.
I = V/R = 10/5 = 2 A
Different Pattern:
If the voltage is doubled while resistance remains constant, what happens to the current?
Current also doubles because I = V/R.
Concept Recognition:
A question gives voltage and resistance but does not mention Ohm's Law. Identify which concept/formula should be applied.
