Trigonometry — Sine, Cosine & Tangent

DEMO LESSON

📐 Trigonometry — Sine, Cosine & Tangent

Trigonometry is the study of relationships between the angles and sides of triangles. Here we focus on a right-angled triangle.

🔺 Step 1 — Understand the Triangle

RIGHT-ANGLED TRIANGLE


       /|
      / |
     /  | Opposite
    / θ  |
  /________|
    Adjacent


Hypotenuse: Side opposite the 90° angle.

🧠 Step 2 — Identify the Three Sides

  • Hypotenuse: Longest side, opposite the 90° angle.
  • Opposite: Side opposite the selected angle θ.
  • Adjacent: Side next to θ, excluding the hypotenuse.

🔵 Sine — SOH

sin θ = Opposite / Hypotenuse

Use sine when the question involves Opposite and Hypotenuse.

🟢 Cosine — CAH

cos θ = Adjacent / Hypotenuse

Use cosine when the question involves Adjacent and Hypotenuse.

🟠 Tangent — TOA

tan θ = Opposite / Adjacent

Use tangent when the question involves Opposite and Adjacent.

🧠 EASY MEMORY TRICK


SOH → Sine = Opposite / Hypotenuse

CAH → Cosine = Adjacent / Hypotenuse

TOA → Tangent = Opposite / Adjacent

📝 Solved Example

In a right-angled triangle, the opposite side is 3 cm and the hypotenuse is 5 cm. Find sin θ.

sin θ = Opposite / Hypotenuse

sin θ = 3 / 5

Answer: sin θ = 3/5

🔄 Different Question Patterns

Pattern 1: Direct formula-based question.
Pattern 2: Triangle diagram is given and student must identify the sides.
Pattern 3: A missing side has to be calculated.
Pattern 4: A real-life situation is given.
Pattern 5: Student must decide which ratio to use.

🎯 Practice Question

A right triangle has an adjacent side of 4 cm and a hypotenuse of 5 cm.

Find cos θ.

cos θ = Adjacent / Hypotenuse = 4/5

⚡ Quick Revision

SOH → sin θ = O/H

CAH → cos θ = A/H

TOA → tan θ = O/A

🔥 STUDY HUB CHALLENGE


Look at the triangle → Identify the sides → Choose the ratio → Solve the problem.

Biology

Heredity — Traits, Genes & Variation

DEMO LESSON

🧬 Heredity — Learn the Concept

Heredity is the transmission of characteristics or traits from parents to their offspring.

🧬 What are Traits?

A trait is a specific characteristic of an organism.

👨 Parent
↓
🧬 Genetic Information
↓
👦 Offspring

Examples of Traits

  • Height
  • Eye colour
  • Hair characteristics
  • Skin colour
  • Shape of seeds in plants

🧬 Genes & Chromosomes

Genes carry information responsible for inherited traits. Genes are located on chromosomes, which are present in the nucleus of cells.

Parents → Chromosomes → Genes → Traits → Offspring

🔬 Variation

Offspring are similar to their parents, but they are not always exactly identical. Differences in characteristics are called variations.

👨‍🔬 Mendel's Experiments

Gregor Mendel studied inheritance using pea plants. His experiments helped establish important principles of inheritance.

🌱 Parent Plant
↓
🧬 Traits
↓
🌱 Offspring

🔄 Different Question Patterns

Pattern 1: Direct definition of heredity.
Pattern 2: Question describes resemblance between parents and offspring.
Pattern 3: Question asks about differences between offspring.
Pattern 4: Diagram or inheritance situation is given.

🧠 Quick Revision

Heredity = Transmission of traits

Gene = Unit of hereditary information

Variation = Differences in characteristics

🎯 Practice Question

What is heredity?

Heredity is the transmission of characteristics or traits from parents to offspring.

Physics

Electric Current — Concepts, Circuits & Problems

DEMO LESSON

⚡ Electric Current — Learn the Concept

Electric current is the flow of electric charge through a conductor.

🔋 Simple Electric Circuit

🔋 Battery
↓
⚡ Current
↓
💡 Bulb
↓
🔋 Battery

Closed Circuit → Current can flow

📌 Important Terms

  • Current: Flow of electric charge.
  • Potential Difference: Work done per unit charge.
  • Resistance: Opposition offered to the flow of current.

⚡ Ohm's Law

V = IR

V = Potential Difference
I = Current
R = Resistance

🧮 Solved Numerical Problem

A resistor of resistance 5 Ω is connected to a 10 V source.

Find the current.

Solution:
I = V/R

I = 10/5

I = 2 A
Answer: 2 A

🔄 Different Question Patterns

Pattern 1: Direct formula question.
Pattern 2: Voltage and resistance are given; calculate current.
Pattern 3: Current and resistance are given; calculate voltage.
Pattern 4: Circuit diagram is given and components must be identified.
Pattern 5: Conceptual situation is given without directly mentioning the formula.

⚠️ Common Mistake

Always check the units.

Voltage → V
Current → A
Resistance → Ω

🧠 Quick Revision

V = IR

I = V/R

R = V/I

🎯 Practice Problem

A resistance of 10 Ω is connected to a 20 V source.

Find the current.

I = V/R = 20/10 = 2 A

Social Studies

Our Constitution and Its Principles

DEMO LESSON

🏛️ Our Constitution and Its Principles

A Constitution provides the basic framework for the functioning of a country and its government.

📖 What is a Constitution?

A Constitution contains fundamental rules, principles and institutions that guide the governance of a country.

🏛️ CONSTITUTION

↓

Government
↓
Rights
↓
Duties
↓
Democratic Principles

🇮🇳 Why is a Constitution Important?

  • Provides a framework for government.
  • Defines powers and responsibilities.
  • Protects important rights.
  • Expresses fundamental values.
  • Supports democratic governance.

⚖️ Key Principles

Democracy
People participate in choosing their representatives.
Equality
All citizens are entitled to equal treatment under the constitutional framework.
Justice
The Constitution aims to promote justice in society.
Liberty
Citizens have important freedoms protected by the constitutional system.
Fraternity
Promotes a sense of unity and brotherhood among citizens.

🔄 Different Question Patterns

Pattern 1: Direct definition question.
Pattern 2: Statement-based question.
Pattern 3: Situation-based question.
Pattern 4: Match principle with its meaning.
Pattern 5: Application-based question.

🧠 Quick Revision

Constitution → Framework → Rights → Duties → Democracy

🎯 Practice Question

Why is a Constitution important for a country?

It provides the basic framework for governance, defines important institutions and principles, and helps protect constitutional rights and values.
