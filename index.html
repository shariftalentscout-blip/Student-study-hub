📐 Trigonometry

Sine • Cosine • Tangent • Triangle Problems

1. Concept

Trigonometry explains the relationship between the sides and angles of a right-angled triangle. 

                 /|
                / |
               /  | Opposite
              /   |
             / θ  |
            /_____|
             Adjacent

          Hypotenuse

2. Important Formulae

sin θ = Opposite / Hypotenuse
cos θ = Adjacent / Hypotenuse
tan θ = Opposite / Adjacent

3. Solved Problem

If opposite = 3 cm and hypotenuse = 5 cm, find sin θ.

sin θ = 3/5

4. Exam Method

LOOK → IDENTIFY → CHOOSE FORMULA → SUBSTITUTE → SOLVE → CHECK

🧬 Heredity

Traits • Genes • Chromosomes • Variation

1. Concept

Heredity is the transmission of characteristics from parents to offspring. 

       PARENTS
          ↓
       CHROMOSOMES
          ↓
         GENES
          ↓
        TRAITS
          ↓
       OFFSPRING

2. Important Terms

  • Heredity: Transmission of traits.
  • Gene: Unit of hereditary information.
  • Variation: Differences in characteristics.
  • Mendel: Pioneer of inheritance studies.

3. Exam Focus

DEFINITION → CONCEPT → DIAGRAM → EXAMPLE → CONCLUSION
