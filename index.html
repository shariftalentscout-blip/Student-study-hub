Mathematics

Trigonometry — Sine, Cosine & Tangent

DEMO LESSON

📐 1. What is Trigonometry?

Trigonometry is the study of the relationship between the sides and angles of triangles. In this lesson we focus on a right-angled triangle.

🔺 2. Understand the Right Triangle

RIGHT-ANGLED TRIANGLE 
             /|
            / |
           /  |  Opposite
          /   |
         / θ  |
        /_____|
         Adjacent

        Hypotenuse

The longest side is the Hypotenuse.

🧠 3. Identify the Three Sides

Hypotenuse:
The longest side of a right-angled triangle, opposite the 90° angle.
Opposite:
The side opposite the selected angle θ.
Adjacent:
The side next to the selected angle θ, excluding the hypotenuse.

🔵 4. Sine — SOH

sin θ = Opposite / Hypotenuse S → O → H

🟢 5. Cosine — CAH

cos θ = Adjacent / Hypotenuse C → A → H

🟠 6. Tangent — TOA

tan θ = Opposite / Adjacent T → O → A

🧠 EASY MEMORY TRICK


SOH → Sine = Opposite / Hypotenuse

CAH → Cosine = Adjacent / Hypotenuse

TOA → Tangent = Opposite / Adjacent

📝 7. Easy Solved Problem

A right-angled triangle has an opposite side of 3 cm and a hypotenuse of 5 cm.

Find sin θ.
sin θ = O/H

= 3/5
Answer: sin θ = 3/5

🧮 8. Medium Problem

A right-angled triangle has an adjacent side of 12 cm and a hypotenuse of 13 cm. Find cos θ.

Step 1: Identify the sides.

Adjacent = 12 cm
Hypotenuse = 13 cm

Step 2:
cos θ = A/H = 12/13
Answer: cos θ = 12/13

🔥 9. Board-Exam Type Problem

In a right-angled triangle ABC, ∠B = 90°. If AB = 8 cm and BC = 6 cm, find the value of tan C.

Thinking Process:
Do not immediately apply a formula.

First identify the angle → C.
Opposite to C → AB = 8 cm.
Adjacent to C → BC = 6 cm.

tan C = Opposite / Adjacent

= 8/6

= 4/3
Answer: tan C = 4/3

🔄 10. Different Question Patterns

Pattern 1: Direct formula-based question.
Pattern 2: Triangle diagram is given and sides must be identified.
Pattern 3: Missing side has to be calculated.
Pattern 4: Angle is changed and the correct sides must be identified again.
Pattern 5: A real-life situation is given.
Pattern 6: Student must decide whether to use sin, cos or tan.

⚠️ 11. Common Mistakes

❌ Do not identify opposite and adjacent without looking at the given angle.

❌ Do not use the hypotenuse as the adjacent side.

❌ Do not memorize only the formula. Understand which sides are required.

❌ Check units carefully in numerical problems.

🎯 12. Practice Questions

Q1. If opposite = 5 cm and hypotenuse = 13 cm, find sin θ.
Q2. If adjacent = 8 cm and hypotenuse = 17 cm, find cos θ.
Q3. If opposite = 9 cm and adjacent = 12 cm, find tan θ.

⚡ 13. Quick Revision

SOH
sin θ = O/H

CAH
cos θ = A/H

TOA
tan θ = O/A

Golden Rule:
First identify the angle. Then identify the three sides. Then choose the ratio.

🏆 14. Study Hub Challenge

LOOK → IDENTIFY → CHOOSE → SUBSTITUTE → SOLVE → CHECK

This is the process to follow before answering a trigonometry problem.

Biology

Heredity — Traits, Genes & Variation

DEMO LESSON

🧬 1. What is Heredity?

Heredity is the transmission of characteristics or traits from parents to offspring.

🧠 2. What are Traits?

A trait is a specific characteristic of an organism.

HEREDITY FLOW
👨 Parent
↓
🧬 Genetic Information
↓
👦 Offspring

🔬 3. Genes and Chromosomes

Genes contain hereditary information. Genes are located on chromosomes. Chromosomes are present in the nucleus of cells.

Chromosome → Gene → Trait

🌱 4. Variation

Offspring resemble their parents but may also show differences. These differences are called variations.

👨‍🔬 5. Mendel

Gregor Mendel conducted experiments on pea plants and studied the inheritance of traits.

🌱 Parent Plants
↓
🧬 Hereditary Information
↓
🌱 Offspring

Similarity + Variation

🔄 6. Question Patterns

Pattern 1: Define heredity.
Pattern 2: Explain variation.
Pattern 3: Explain the role of genes.
Pattern 4: Explain Mendel's experiments.
Pattern 5: Diagram or situation-based question.

⚠️ 7. Common Mistakes

❌ Do not confuse genes with chromosomes.

❌ Do not write variation as complete absence of similarity between parents and offspring.

❌ Use correct biological terminology.

🎯 8. Practice Question

What is heredity?

Heredity is the transmission of characteristics or traits from parents to offspring.
QUICK REVISION

Heredity → Transmission of traits
Gene → Hereditary information
Variation → Differences in characteristics
Mendel → Pea plant experiments

Physics

Electric Current — Concepts, Circuits & Problems

DEMO LESSON

⚡ 1. What is Electric Current?

Electric current is the flow of electric charge through a conductor.

🔋 2. Simple Electric Circuit

SIMPLE CIRCUIT
🔋 Battery
↓
⚡ Current
↓
💡 Bulb
↓
🔋 Battery

Closed circuit → Current can flow

📌 3. Important Terms

Electric Current:
Rate of flow of electric charge.
Potential Difference:
Work done per unit charge.
Resistance:
Opposition offered to the flow of electric current.

⚡ 4. Important Formula

I = Q/t Current = Charge / Time

⚡ 5. Ohm's Law

V = IR V = Potential Difference | I = Current | R = Resistance

🧮 6. Easy Numerical

Resistance = 5 Ω
Voltage = 10 V

Find current.

I = V/R

I = 10/5

I = 2 A
Answer: 2 A

🧮 7. Medium Numerical

A current of 3 A flows through a resistor of 4 Ω. Find the potential difference.

Given:
I = 3 A
R = 4 Ω

V = IR

V = 3 × 4

V = 12 V
Answer: 12 V

🔥 8. Board-Exam Type Numerical

A potential difference of 20 V is applied across a resistor of resistance 10 Ω. Calculate the current flowing through it.

Step 1: Write the formula.

I = V/R
Step 2: Substitute the values.

I = 20/10 = 2 A
Answer: 2 A

🔄 9. Different Question Patterns

Pattern 1: Direct definition.
Pattern 2: Formula-based numerical.
Pattern 3: Circuit diagram.
Pattern 4: Unit conversion.
Pattern 5: Application-based problem.

⚠️ 10. Common Mistakes

❌ Always write the formula first.

❌ Check whether the units are correct.

❌ Do not confuse current, voltage and resistance.

❌ Write the final answer with the correct unit.

🎯 11. Practice Problems

Q1. A 10 Ω resistor is connected to a 20 V source. Find current.
Q2. A current of 2 A flows through a 5 Ω resistor. Find voltage.
Q3. A charge of 20 C flows through a conductor in 5 seconds. Find current.

⚡ 12. Quick Revision

I = Q/t

V = IR

I = V/R

R = V/I

Current → Ampere (A)
Voltage → Volt (V)
Resistance → Ohm (Ω)

Social Studies

Our Constitution and Its Principles

DEMO LESSON

🏛️ 1. What is a Constitution?

A Constitution provides the basic framework for the functioning of a country and its government.

🏛️ CONSTITUTION
↓
Government
↓
Rights & Duties
↓
Democratic Principles

📖 2. Why is a Constitution Important?

  • Provides a framework for government.
  • Defines powers and responsibilities.
  • Protects important rights.
  • Expresses fundamental values.
  • Supports democratic governance.

⚖️ 3. Key Principles

Democracy
People participate in choosing their representatives.
Equality
Citizens are entitled to equal treatment under the constitutional framework.
Justice
The constitutional system aims to promote justice in society.
Liberty
Important freedoms are protected within the constitutional framework.
Fraternity
Promotes unity and brotherhood among citizens.

🔄 4. Different Question Patterns

Pattern 1: Direct definition.
Pattern 2: Explain a constitutional principle.
Pattern 3: Statement-based question.
Pattern 4: Match principle with meaning.
Pattern 5: Situation/application-based question.

⚠️ 5. Common Mistakes

❌ Avoid writing only one-word answers when explanation is required.

❌ Use key constitutional terms.

❌ Structure long answers with points.

📝 6. Answer Writing Method

Step 1: Start with the definition or introduction.
Step 2: Write the important points.
Step 3: Give an example when appropriate.
Step 4: Finish with a short conclusion.

🎯 7. Practice Question

Why is a Constitution important for a country?

A Constitution provides the basic framework for governance, defines important institutions and principles, and helps protect constitutional rights and values.
QUICK REVISION

Constitution → Framework
Democracy → People's participation
Equality → Equal treatment
Justice → Fairness
Liberty → Freedom
Fraternity → Unity
