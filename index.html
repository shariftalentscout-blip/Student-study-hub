DEMO LESSON

📐 Trigonometry — Sine, Cosine & Tangent

Trigonometry is the study of relationships between the angles and sides of triangles. In this lesson we focus on a right-angled triangle.

🔺 Step 1 — Understand the Triangle

RIGHT-ANGLED TRIANGLE


       /|
      / |
     /  | Opposite
    / θ  |
  /________|
    Adjacent


Hypotenuse: Side opposite the 90° angle and the longest side.

🧠 Step 2 — Identify the Three Sides

  • Hypotenuse: Longest side, opposite the 90° angle.
  • Opposite: Side opposite the selected angle θ.
  • Adjacent: Side next to θ, excluding the hypotenuse.

🔵 Sine — SOH

sin θ = Opposite / Hypotenuse

Use sine when the question involves Opposite and Hypotenuse.

🟢 Cosine — CAH

cos θ = Adjacent / Hypotenuse

Use cosine when the question involves Adjacent and Hypotenuse.

🟠 Tangent — TOA

tan θ = Opposite / Adjacent

Use tangent when the question involves Opposite and Adjacent.

🧠 EASY MEMORY TRICK


SOH → Sine = Opposite / Hypotenuse

CAH → Cosine = Adjacent / Hypotenuse

TOA → Tangent = Opposite / Adjacent

📝 Solved Example

In a right-angled triangle, the opposite side is 3 cm and the hypotenuse is 5 cm. Find sin θ.

sin θ = Opposite / Hypotenuse

sin θ = 3 / 5

Answer: sin θ = 3/5

🔄 Different Question Patterns

Pattern 1: Direct formula-based question.
Pattern 2: Triangle diagram is given and student must identify the sides.
Pattern 3: A missing side has to be calculated.
Pattern 4: A real-life situation is given.
Pattern 5: Student must decide which trigonometric ratio to use.

🎯 Practice Question

A right triangle has an adjacent side of 4 cm and a hypotenuse of 5 cm.

Find cos θ.
cos θ = Adjacent / Hypotenuse = 4/5

⚡ Quick Revision

SOH → sin θ = O/H

CAH → cos θ = A/H

TOA → tan θ = O/A

🔥 STUDY HUB CHALLENGE


Look at the triangle → Identify the sides → Choose the ratio → Solve the problem.

Biology

Heredity — Traits, Genes & Variation

DEMO LESSON

🧬 Heredity — Learn the Concept

Heredity is the transmission of characteristics or traits from parents to their offspring.

🧬 What are Traits?

A trait is a specific characteristic of an organism.

👨 Parent
↓
🧬 Genetic Information
↓
👦 Offspring

Examples of Traits

  • Height
  • Eye colour
  • Hair characteristics
  • Skin colour
  • Shape of seeds in plants

🧬 Genes & Chromosomes

Genes carry information responsible for inherited traits. Genes are located on chromosomes, which are present in the nucleus of cells.

Parents → Chromosomes → Genes → Traits → Offspring

🔬 Variation

Offspring are similar to their parents, but they are not always exactly identical. Differences in characteristics are called variations.

👨‍🔬 Mendel's Experiments

Gregor Mendel studied inheritance using pea plants. His experiments helped establish important principles of inheritance.

↓
🧬 Traits
↓
🌱 Offspring

🔄 Different Question Patterns

Pattern 1: Direct definition of heredity.
Pattern 2: A question describes resemblance between parents and offspring.
Pattern 3: A question asks about differences between offspring.
Pattern 4: A diagram or inheritance situation is given.

🧠 Quick Revision

Heredity = Transmission of traits

Gene = Unit of hereditary information

Variation = Differences in characteristics

Physics

Electric Current — Concepts, Circuits & Problems

DEMO LESSON

⚡ Electric Current — Learn the Concept

Electric current is the flow of electric charge through a conductor.

🔋 Simple Electric Circuit

🔋 Battery
↓
⚡ Current
↓
💡 Bulb
↓
🔋 Battery

Closed Circuit → Current can flow

📌 Important Terms

  • Current: Flow of electric charge.
  • Potential Difference: Work done per unit charge.
  • Resistance: Opposition offered to the flow of current.

⚡ Ohm's Law

V = IR

V = Potential Difference
I = Current
R = Resistance

🧮 Solved Numerical Problem

A resistor of resistance 5 Ω is connected to a 10 V source.

Find the current.

Solution:
I = V/R

I = 10/5

I = 2 A
Answer: 2 A

🔄 Different Question Patterns

Pattern 1: Direct formula question.
Pattern 2: Voltage and resistance are given; student must calculate current.
Pattern 3: Current and resistance are given; student must calculate voltage.
Pattern 4: A circuit diagram is given and student must identify the components.
Pattern 5: A conceptual situation is given without directly mentioning Ohm's Law.

⚠️ Common Mistake

Always check the units.

Voltage → V
Current → A
Resistance → Ω

🧠 Quick Revision

V = IR

I = V/R

R = V/I

🎯 Practice Problem

A resistance of 10 Ω is connected to a 20 V source.

Find the current.
I = V/R = 20/10 = 2 A
